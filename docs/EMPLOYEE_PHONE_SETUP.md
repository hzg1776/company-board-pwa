# How to Put the Employee App on Your Phone

Use these steps to put the employee app on your Home Screen and turn on notifications.

## Website

[Open the employee website](https://itotexpress.com/palzivalerts/employee)

## iPhone

1. Open **Safari**.
2. Go to the employee website above.
3. Sign in with your employee username and password.
4. Tap the **Share** button. It looks like a square with an arrow pointing up.
5. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
6. Tap **Add**.
7. Go back to your Home Screen.
8. Tap the new **Alert Center** app icon.
9. Sign in again if asked.
10. Tap **Sign up for alerts**.
11. Tap **Allow** when your iPhone asks about notifications.

**Important:** On an iPhone, open the app from the new Home Screen icon before signing up for alerts. Do not finish notification setup inside the Safari tab.

## Android

1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Go to the employee website above.
3. Sign in with your employee username and password.
4. Tap the **three dots** in the top-right corner.
5. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
6. Tap **Install** or **Add** to confirm.
7. Go back to your Home Screen.
8. Tap the new **Alert Center** app icon.
9. Sign in again if asked.
10. Tap **Sign up for alerts**.
11. Tap **Allow** when Android asks about notifications.

## If You Accidentally Blocked Notifications

### iPhone

1. Open the phone's **Settings**.
2. Tap **Notifications**.
3. Tap **Alert Center**.
4. Turn on **Allow Notifications**.
5. Open **Alert Center** from the Home Screen again.

### Android

1. Open the phone's **Settings**.
2. Tap **Apps**.
3. Tap **Alert Center**.
4. Tap **Notifications**.
5. Turn notifications on.
6. Open **Alert Center** from the Home Screen again.

If **Sign up for alerts** appears, tap it again. If notifications still do not work, ask HR or Systems for help.
